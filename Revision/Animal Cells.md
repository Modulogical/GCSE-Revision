
**Contains** 
- A [[Cell Membrane]], which separates the inside of the cell from its environment outside. It's a **partially permeable membrane**
- A [[Nucleus]], which **controls cell activities** and **contains chromosomes** needed for reproduction
- [[Ribosomes]], which are **the site of protein synthesis**
- [[Cytoplasm]], a jelly-like substance and **the site of chemical reactions** inside the cell
- [[Mitochondria]], which is **the site of aerobic respiration**
